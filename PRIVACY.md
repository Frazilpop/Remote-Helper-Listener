# Privacy Policy — Remote Helper

_Last updated: 22 July 2026_

Remote Helper lets you use your iPhone or iPad as a wireless keyboard for
your own computers over your local network. In short: **Remote Helper
collects nothing, sends nothing to its developer, and keeps everything on
your own devices and network.**

This policy covers both the Remote Helper iOS app and the Remote Helper
listener that runs on your computer.

## What we collect

Nothing. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising,
no tracking, and no third-party services or SDKs. The developer does not
receive any data from the app or the listener.

## What the app stores on your device

- **A random device identifier**, created the first time you open the app
  and kept in the iOS keychain. It lets a computer remember you've paired
  with it. It is a random value — not linked to your name, Apple ID, phone
  number, or any other identity — and it is never sent to the developer or
  any third party.
- **Your preferences:** the names and mascots you give your computers, any
  addresses you add by hand, and which computer is currently selected.

These live only in the app's local storage on your device.

## What the listener stores on your computer

- The identifiers and display names of devices you've paired, so you don't
  have to pair them again.
- A local diagnostic log.

Both are kept only on that computer (in its local application-data folder)
and are never transmitted anywhere.

## What is sent over the network

When you type or press keys, the resulting text and key presses are sent
**directly to the computer you have selected, over your local network
only.** They are not sent to the developer or any third party. Because
this travels over your local network without encryption, use Remote Helper
only on networks you trust (such as your own home network).

## Dictation

If you use the keyboard's microphone/dictation button, your speech is
transcribed by Apple's built-in iOS dictation, which is governed by
[Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) — not by
Remote Helper. The app only receives the resulting text, exactly as if you
had typed it. Remote Helper does not itself record audio or request
microphone access.

## Permissions the app uses

- **Local Network** — required, so the app can find your computers and send
  keystrokes to them. This is the only permission the app requests.

## Children

Remote Helper is a general-purpose utility. It does not target children and
does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
